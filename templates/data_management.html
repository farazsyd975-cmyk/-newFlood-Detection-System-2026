Preprocessing & Validation Rules

Automated data cleansing & missing-value handling

1
Missing Value Imputation:

Missing atmospheric fields are automatically imputed using baseline seasonal medians for that specific Punjab district.

2
Feature Bounds Sanitization:

Inputs are clipped to physically plausible hydrologic boundaries (e.g. soil moisture: 5–100%, humidity: 10–100%).

3
StandardScaler Normalization:

Raw parameters pass through the serialized StandardScaler inside the Random Forest pipeline before scoring.

4
Audit Trail Persistence:

Every processed prediction is indexed in SQLite (database/flood.db) with timestamp and telemetry tags.
