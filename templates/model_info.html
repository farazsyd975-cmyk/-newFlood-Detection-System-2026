Explainable AI (XAI) & Engineering Rationale

Transparent decision boundaries for disaster management

1. How is Cause-Aware Classification achieved?

A specialized Random Forest classifier (cause_model.pkl) maps non-linear feature interactions to isolate whether the flood hazard stems from: (a) Infiltration excess rainfall downpours, (b) Storm channel drainage choking, (c) River embankment surges from dam discharge, or (d) Catastrophic combined multi-factor events.

2. Why does 24h rainfall and antecedent moisture dominate?

In Punjab's alluvial floodplains, runoff physics adheres to USDA SCS Curve Number theory: once antecedent rain ($R_{7d}$) saturates the upper soil column, infiltration rate drops toward zero. Any subsequent 24-hour rainfall ($R_{24}$) converts nearly 100% into overland flow and channel surcharge.

3. How are missing values and offline states handled?

The platform operates a fail-safe dual-mode ingestion service. If the Open-Meteo API is unreachable or times out, the backend injects calibrated historical district seasonal medians, marking the data provenance explicitly as "Calibrated Regional Baseline".
